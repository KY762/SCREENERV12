## 1. The question

Does a stock underperform a same-day pool of other names in the three
trading days after its IPO lock-up expires? Source: Field & Hanka (2001),
*Journal of Finance*, "The Expiration of IPO Share Lockups" -- 1,948
lock-up expirations, 1988-1997, roughly -1.5% abnormal return over the
three trading days around expiry. Full reasoning, the feasibility check,
and the literature this project's own `net_share_issuance` screen cannot
reach: `docs/12`.

## 2. The configuration

`docs/12` sections 3-4, unchanged:

```
CANDIDATES  symbols with a first stored bar in (2010-02-01, 2015-06-30]
IDENTIFY    SEC EDGAR full text search, by ticker, 424B1-424B5 filings
            within 15 days of the first bar; confirmed only if the
            filing's own cover page states the same ticker and its
            "Lock-Up Agreement" clause states one unambiguous day count
HORIZON     3 trading days (Field & Hanka's own event window)
TRADE       the engine's rules: next open, refused on a zero-volume bar;
            exit at the open 3 sessions later, a zero-volume bar's open,
            or a series' last close (diagnostics.screen.trade_return,
            reused unchanged)
POOL        the same full equity universe the candidates were drawn from,
            same calendar entry dates (not swing_small_mid -- see docs/12
            section 4 for why)
COST        each name's own measured spread (calc/spread.spread_table)
STATISTIC   per event, net return minus the same-day pool's mean net
            return; mean of that gap across events, its t, and the
            one-sided p for the gap being NEGATIVE
```

This is a diagnostic configuration, like H8's -- no hold, stop or entry
rule of its own to tune. Unlike H8, it is not run through `screener
diagnose screen`; `diagnostics/lockup_screen.py` is its own module and
this file registers no `backtest run` or `diagnose_screen` options, which
is why the `[[validation]]`/`[[test]]` arrays below stay empty rather than
naming a configuration shape the loader would try to validate against
`backtest run`'s options.

## 3. What passes on development (docs/12 section 5)

1. Net gap one-sided p ≤ 0.05 (negative).
2. Negative in ≥ 2 of development's 3 regime buckets.
3. The 10%-trimmed gap negative too.

All three, written before `screener ingest-lockups` or the return
measurement has been run.
