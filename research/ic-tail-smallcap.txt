IC is the linear edge: a real equity factor sits near 0.02-0.05 with |t| above 
3, and anything past 0.10 on liquid names is a lookahead bug until proven 
otherwise. Tail held is the share of the biggest subsequent movers that sat in 
the indicator's top decile, and lift is that against chance -- 1.00x is no 
skill. For a rule that cuts losers small and lets winners run, lift is the 
number that pays and IC is the distraction. Spread is the top decile's mean 
forward return minus everything else. Forward returns are open-to-open from the
session AFTER the signal, and windows do not overlap.
